# Privacy Policy for Tetris New Tab

**Last updated:** September 13, 2026

Tetris New Tab is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension operates entirely client-side inside your browser and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

---

### Data Collection & Usage
- **No Personal Data Collected**: Tetris New Tab does not collect names, emails, IP addresses, browsing history, or search queries.
- **Local Storage Only**: The extension uses local browser storage (`chrome.storage.local` / `localStorage`) strictly to store:
  - Game high score and statistics
  - Custom quick-launch shortcuts added by the user
  - User preferences (Sound muted status, Light/Dark theme mode, preferred search engine)
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking**: We do not use any third-party tracking scripts, telemetry, or analytics services.
- **No Data Transmission**: No user data is ever sent to external servers or sold to third parties.

---

### Permissions
- **`storage`**: Used solely to persist your game scores and custom settings locally on your device.
